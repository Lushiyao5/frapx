The raymarching helpers in @frapx/glsl-raymarch are adapted from shaders by
Inigo Quilez published on Shadertoy. Each source shader states the MIT License
in its header:

- Raymarching - Primitives (2013) https://www.shadertoy.com/view/Xds3zN
  raymarch loop, calcNormal (tetrahedron technique), calcAO, and setCamera
  (lookAt, cameraRay)
- Soft Shadow Variation.1 (2018) https://www.shadertoy.com/view/lsKcDD
  softShadow (improved technique)

frapx_softShadow adds a fallback to the plain estimate when consecutive
samples do not intersect, and frapx_ao normalizes its result by the sample
count. The techniques are explained in his articles, which state no license
of their own:

- https://iquilezles.org/articles/rmshadows/
- https://iquilezles.org/articles/normalsSDF/
