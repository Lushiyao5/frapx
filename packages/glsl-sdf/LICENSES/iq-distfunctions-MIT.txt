The distance functions in @frapx/glsl-sdf are adapted from shaders by
Inigo Quilez published on Shadertoy. Each source shader states the MIT License
in its header:

- Raymarching - Primitives (2013) https://www.shadertoy.com/view/Xds3zN
  sdSphere, sdBox (3D), sdTorus, sdCylinder, sdCapsule, sdPlane, sdHexPrism,
  and sdHexagon (2D cross-section of sdHexPrism)
- Triangle - distance 2D (2014) https://www.shadertoy.com/view/XsXSz4
  sdTriangle
- Rounded Box - distance 2D (2015) https://www.shadertoy.com/view/4llXD7
  sdRoundBox (2D), and sdBox (2D) as its zero-radius case
- Rhombus - distance 2D (2017) https://www.shadertoy.com/view/XdXcRB
  sdRhombus
- Regular Triangle - distance 2D (2017) https://www.shadertoy.com/view/Xl2yDW
  sdEquilateralTriangle
- Combination SDF (2018) https://www.shadertoy.com/view/lt3BW2
  sdRoundBox (3D), opUnion, opSubtract, opIntersect, opSmoothUnion,
  opSmoothSubtract, opSmoothIntersect
- Pie - distance 2D (2019) https://www.shadertoy.com/view/3l23RK
  sdPie
- Arc - distance (2019) https://www.shadertoy.com/view/wl23RK
  sdArc
- Cone - distance 3D (2019) https://www.shadertoy.com/view/tsSXzK
  sdCappedCone
- Octahedron - distance (2019) https://www.shadertoy.com/view/wsSGDG
  sdOctahedron
- Ellipsoid - distance (2019) https://www.shadertoy.com/view/tdS3DG
  sdEllipsoid
- Disk - distance 2D (2020) https://www.shadertoy.com/view/3ltSW2
  sdCircle
- Segment - distance 2D (2020) https://www.shadertoy.com/view/3tdSDj
  sdSegment
- Box Frame - distance 3D (2020) https://www.shadertoy.com/view/3ljcRh
  sdBoxFrame

The formulas are explained in his articles, which state no license of their
own. opRound, opOnion and the domain operators (rotate, repeat, twist, bend)
follow the elementary formulas described there:

- https://iquilezles.org/articles/distfunctions/
- https://iquilezles.org/articles/distfunctions2d/
